Privacy Policy

Arrow Escape · Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Arrow Escape game ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Arrow Escape is developed by Sarfraz Khan. Contact: YOUR-EMAIL@example.com

Information we collect

  • Account information (optional): if you create an account we collect your email address, the player name you choose and your password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are stored in protected (hashed) form. We cannot see your password.
  • Game data: your progress, coins, hints, unlocked themes and music, and settings. If you are logged in this is saved in the cloud so you can continue on another device. If you play as a guest it stays on your phone.
  • Advertising data: the app shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone data.

How we use information

  • to let you log in and keep your game progress safe;
  • to let you reset your password by email;
  • to show rewarded ads, which keep the game free;
  • to fix problems and improve the game.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by service providers that help us run the app: Supabase (accounts and cloud save) and Google AdMob (ads). They process data under their own privacy policies.

Data storage and security

Account and cloud save data is stored with Supabase. We use reasonable measures to protect it, but no online service is 100% secure.

Your choices

  • You can play as a guest without giving any personal information.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and saved data by emailing us from the email you registered with. We will delete it within a reasonable time.
  • You can limit ad personalisation in your phone's Settings (Google → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child gave us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
